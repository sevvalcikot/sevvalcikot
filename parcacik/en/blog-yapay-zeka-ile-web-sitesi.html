How I built my personal website with AI

From idea to launch: how I built my sea-themed site, admin panel and assistant together with AI.

Categories
  • Web Design
  • Artificial Intelligence
Tags
  • #HTML
  • #CSS

  • #Claude

This site was made for a "website that introduces yourself" assignment. Instead of using a ready-made template, I wanted to create my own design by working together with AI (Claude).

The idea: a dive into the Mediterranean

My first attempts looked too ordinary. Since I study at Akdeniz (Mediterranean) University, I tied the concept to the sea: scrolling down the page means diving into the sea. As you scroll the water gets darker, a depth and temperature gauge changes in the corner, fish swim away from the cursor and bubbles rise. The contact section sits at the very bottom, on the seabed.

Technologies I used

  1. HTML5 and CSS3: the structure of the pages, glass-like cards and a layout that works on phones and tablets (responsive).
  2. JavaScript: light patterns and fish drawn on canvas, and a half-submerged title made with an SVG filter.
  3. Supabase: the database and login for the admin panel.
  4. GitHub Pages: free hosting for the site.

Admin panel and assistant

I also added my own content management system (CMS). From the panel I can change projects, the gallery, blog posts and my about text without touching code. The same panel has a calendar, tasks, notes and a small assistant that answers Turkish questions like "what do I have tomorrow?".

Working with AI

AI didn't do everything for me; I had to explain what I wanted, test the results and ask again where I didn't like them. Many decisions, from gallery titles to colours, were mine. Most of all, this process taught me to describe an idea clearly and step by step.
